Frequently Asked Questions

Can we increase frequency only for the holiday season?

Yes. Retail accounts commonly run a higher frequency from November through early January and drop back afterward. We adjust the schedule without renegotiating the whole contract.

Do you work around production shifts?

Yes. For manufacturing accounts we clean between shifts or during planned downtime so we never stop a line.
