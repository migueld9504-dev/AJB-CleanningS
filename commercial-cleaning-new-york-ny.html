Manhattan cleaning is governed by the building, not the tenant. Freight elevator windows, certificate-of-insurance requirements, union rules and nightly access lists shape what is actually possible — so the first conversation is usually with building management, not about mops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you provide a certificate of insurance for a Manhattan building?

Yes. We issue COIs naming the building and managing agent as required, which is normally the gating item before any vendor is allowed to work after hours.

Do you handle buildings with union labor requirements?

Building rules vary and some Manhattan properties restrict outside vendors. We confirm the building's vendor policy before quoting, so you do not sign a contract we cannot legally perform.
