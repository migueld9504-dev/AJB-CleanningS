Our Specialized 3-Phase Post-Construction Process

Post-construction cleanup is not just standard wiping; it requires surgical precision. Our local teams execute a strict three-phase protocol to eliminate hazardous silica dust, plaster splatter, and paint residue:

  • Phase 1: Rough Clean (El Desbaste): Clearing major trash, remaining stickers, protective plastic, and initial floor sweep to prepare the space for detail work.
  • Phase 2: Final Detail Clean (Limpieza de Detalle): Deep sanitization of all surfaces, detailed vacuuming, window track cleaning, glass scraping, and detailed floor treatments.
  • Phase 3: Touch-Up Clean (El Repaso): A final sweep performed right before the official client walkthrough to remove any settling dust or footprints.
