New Haven's economy runs on healthcare, research and education. The medical district, the laboratory space around Science Park and the professional offices downtown all demand documented protocols — in a lab or clinical setting, cleaning is a compliance activity, not housekeeping.

What we clean in New Haven

  • Clinics and outpatient facilities: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1030 protocols, terminal cleaning and color-coded systems to prevent cross-contamination.
  • Laboratory and research suites: Dust-free, anti-static cleaning with strict rules about which surfaces we do and do not touch.
  • Downtown professional offices: Nightly service for firms around the Green and Chapel Street.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you follow written protocols for clinical spaces?

Yes. Clinical accounts get a documented protocol covering products, dwell times, color-coded equipment and the order of rooms, so the work is auditable rather than assumed.

Can you work inside a laboratory?

We clean the areas a lab designates as cleanable, following their instructions on restricted surfaces and equipment. We do not touch instrumentation or active work areas.
